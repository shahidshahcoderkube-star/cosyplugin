SECTION 1: BOOKING & PAYMENT NOTIFICATIONS (EMAILS 1 - 6)
================================================================================

1. CUSTOMER BOOKING CONFIRMATION EMAIL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Sent To: Customer (Buyer)
- Trigger Event: Triggered immediately when customer completes checkout & WorldPay payment is successful.
- Email Subject: 🌸 Thank You for Your Booking with CosyChats
- Header Title: Booking Confirmation
- Content Paragraphs:
  "Hello [Customer Name],"
  "Thank you for booking a conversation through CosyChats."
  "We're delighted you've chosen CosyChats, and we hope you enjoy your upcoming conversation."
  "Please find your booking confirmation below."
- Data Fields / Table 1 (Booking Information Summary):
  • Order ID (#365)
  • Experience Booked (Service Title, e.g., Kids)
  • Service Provider (Provider Display Name)
  • Customer Name
  • Customer Email
  • Gift Recipient Name & Email (if booked as gift)
  • Start Date (e.g., 13-08-2026)
  • End Date (e.g., 19-08-2026)
  • Weekly Schedule (e.g., 1 Week Duration)
  • Number of Weeks (e.g., 1)
  • Week Days (e.g., Thursday)
  • Total Booked Slots (e.g., 6 slots)
  • Selected Slots (e.g., 12:00 PM, 12:10 PM, 12:20 PM, 12:30 PM, 12:40 PM, 12:50 PM)
- Data Fields / Table 2 (Payment Details):
  • Experience Cost (£9.00)
  • Service Fee* (£0.27)
  • Total Paid (£9.27) [Highlighted Row: #fdf2fb background, #a44390 text]
- Footnote: "*A small non-refundable fee to help us run our platform safely & smoothly."

  • Weekly Schedule & Week Days

  • Selected Slots Timeline
- Data Fields / Table 2 (Financial Details):
  • Provider Revenue Share (£9.00)
  • Service Fee* (Net) (£0.27)
  • Total Paid (£9.27) [Highlighted Row]
- Data Fields / Table 3 (WorldPay Payment Gateway Details):
  • Payment Gateway (WorldPay)
